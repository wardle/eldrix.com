Eldrix makes PatientCare, an electronic health and care record, and open-source tools for health and care data. It was founded in 2012 by Dr Mark Wardle, a consultant neurologist and clinical informatician.

Our software is used operationally in the NHS, and in a wide range of local, national and international clinical research projects. It was used worldwide during the COVID-19 pandemic.
