Eldrix Ltd was founded in 2012 by Dr Mark Wardle to build electronic patient record software.

Mark is a consultant neurologist and clinical informatician in Cardiff. He is a Fellow of the British Computer Society, and was a founding fellow of the Faculty of Clinical Informatics. He writes about health informatics at [wardle.org](https://wardle.org).

## How we build software

Applications ask for data in the shape they need, and separate services provide it. Those services are built on health and care standards: SNOMED CT, the NHS dictionary of medicines and devices (dm+d), HL7 FHIR and NHS organisational data. Much of that foundation is [open source](/software/#open-source).
