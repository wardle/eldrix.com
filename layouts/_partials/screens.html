{{- /* Context: a product page bundle. Renders front matter `screens`: each has `src` (path inside the bundle), `title`, `caption`, `alt` and optional `device` (`phone`, or `document` for a page of a document). An optional `file` (path inside the bundle) is offered as a download, labelled by `file_title`; the image then links to the file. Entries whose image is missing are skipped, so the section only appears once screenshots exist.
